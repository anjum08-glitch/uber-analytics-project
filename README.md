# uber-analytics-project
 Uber Analytics Dashboard – End-to-End Insight for Smarter Decisions
I created an interactive Uber Power BI Dashboard to analyze rider behavior, vehicle performance, revenue trends, and customer patterns. This dashboard helps understand business performance with clear, actionable insights.
🔹 Home Page
A clean landing page that introduces the project and provides quick navigation to:
 Overview | Vehicle | Revenue | Rider
🔹 Overview Dashboard
Provides a full business snapshot:
93K Total Bookings
57K Lost Bookings
₹52M Total Revenue
2.51M KM Distance Travelled
24.64 KM Avg Distance per Trip
Key insights:
Monthly booking trend across the year
Revenue distribution by vehicle type (Auto, Bike, Go Mini, Go Sedan, etc.)
Top-performing locations
Average customer & driver ratings
🔹 Vehicle Performance Dashboard
Deep dive into vehicle-wise metrics:
Revenue, customer count, and completed bookings
Monthly booking trend for each vehicle type
Comparison of vehicle segments like Auto, Bike, Go Mini, Go Sedan & Premier Sedan
Total revenue contribution: ₹51.8M
This helps identify which vehicle types drive the most value.
🔹 Revenue Dashboard
Breakdown of earnings by:
Monthly & Quarterly Revenue
Payment Methods (UPI, Cash, Wallet, Card)
Top Customers by Revenue
Revenue by vehicle categories
UPI emerges as the most preferred payment method with the highest transaction volume.
🔹 Rider Insights Dashboard
Complete customer analytics:
104K total customers reached
Monthly & quarterly customer trends
Customer-level details: revenue, rides completed, lost rides, total distance
Cancelled ride reasons (Driver, Customer, Incomplete)
This helps understand rider patterns and improve service quality.
